What a pipe repair visit may involve

The exact work depends on the location and condition of the pipe. A plumber needs to identify the source of the trouble before deciding whether a repair or a more involved plumbing solution makes sense. After assessing the situation, the next steps can be discussed with you.

A short checklist before the visit:
- Make a note of the affected room or area and any visible water.
- Keep belongings clear of the work area where possible.
- Tell us if the leak is ongoing or has changed since you first noticed it.
- Mention any repair attempts or changes to the plumbing already made.

One mistake to avoid is assuming a damp spot marks the exact place where a pipe is damaged. Water can show up away from its source, so describe what you see rather than cutting into a wall to investigate. If you see a sudden or worsening leak, call promptly.
